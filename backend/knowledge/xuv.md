# AUTONEX XUV · Adventure concept demonstration guide

## G-Wagon-inspired design - AUTONEX demonstration fixture
AUTONEX XUV is a fictional electric adventure concept. Its boxy upright silhouette, flat roof, round headlights, squared wheel arches, roof rails and rear-mounted spare wheel evoke the G-Wagon design style. It is not a Mercedes-Benz vehicle or licensed factory model. No manufacturer partnership or system access is claimed.

## Adventure and performance - AUTONEX demonstration fixture
Adventure is the base demo trim, with an illustrative USD 89,900 estimate, 450 hp and AWD. Adventure Performance uses an illustrative USD 109,900 estimate and 620 hp. Both are fictional configurations. The demo catalog uses 280 miles of range and a 32-minute 10-80 percent charging time solely for testing the application. These figures do not describe a real G-Class, Mercedes-Benz or other manufacturer's vehicle.

## Configuration and ownership - AUTONEX demonstration fixture
Choose burgundy, black, white, sand or blue paint; 20-inch or 22-inch demo wheels; and a tan or black cabin option. Optional Adventure, Comfort and Technology packages change the illustrative estimate. All options are mock fixtures. Service and test-drive actions are local demonstrations, not appointments or bookings. The XUV is the default dashboard vehicle; BMW i4 and BMW iX remain selectable.
