# BMW i4 · Reference notes and demonstration guide

## Public BMW reference notes
Version: 2026 model-year announcement, published November 6, 2025. Region: United States. BMW described the i4 eDrive40 as exceeding 300 miles of estimated range with either its 18-inch or 19-inch wheels, using silicon-carbide inverters. The release lists the i4 M60 at 593 hp in Sport mode and 3.6 seconds from 0 to 60 mph. Range figures in this announcement are preliminary manufacturer estimates, not a guarantee of real-world range.
Source: https://newsroom.bmwgroup.com/americas/2026-bmw-i4-update-measures

## Everyday electric performance - AUTONEX demonstration fixture
The app's BMW i4 catalog configuration is a fictional demonstration fixture, not BMW specifications. Comfort, Performance and Competition are demo trim labels. Demo performance values, USD estimates, paint options, packages and charging times exist only to exercise comparison and configuration workflows. The demo gran-coupe can be configured for sporty daily driving or value. Do not attribute these fixture values to a real BMW vehicle.

## Comfort and technology - AUTONEX demonstration fixture
The demo Comfort Package contains comfort-oriented options. The demo Technology Package groups assistance features. The demo Performance Package represents track-oriented options. Actual BMW equipment requires verification with an authorized source for the exact model year and region.

## Charging guidance
Charging depends on battery temperature, state of charge and charging equipment. The catalog's 18-minute charging time and 340-mile range are demonstration values. They are not the figures in the public BMW announcement above.
