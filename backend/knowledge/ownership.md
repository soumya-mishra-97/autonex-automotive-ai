# Ownership · Pricing and test drives

## Transparent estimates
All AUTONEX vehicles, specifications and prices are fictional demonstration data in USD. Configuration estimates include the trim and selected options. Tax, registration, delivery and dealer fees are excluded. Estimates are not binding offers. Configuration tools validate option compatibility and calculate totals directly from the catalog.

## Choosing a vehicle
A gran-coupe suits drivers who prioritize a low seating position and a sportier driving experience. An SUV offers more flexibility for family travel. For a limited budget, choose the base trim and standard options before adding packages. The configurator can change paint, wheels, interior and packages independently.

## Requesting a test drive
A test-drive request records a preferred date and contact details. It does not confirm a reservation or send an email. A dealer integration and confirmation workflow would be required for actual scheduling. Never provide payment details to the concierge.
