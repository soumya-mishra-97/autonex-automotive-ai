## Family and space - AUTONEX demonstration fixture
The app's BMW iX configuration is a fictional demonstration fixture. Family and M Sport are demo trim labels. All displayed range, seating packages, charging times, USD estimates and configurable equipment in this app's catalog are illustrative. The demo Family Package contains third-row seating for workflow testing; this does not claim third-row seating for a real BMW iX. Never use this fixture to infer real vehicle seating or specifications.
